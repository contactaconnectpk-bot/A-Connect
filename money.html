💰 Money

Money can be confusing when you're just starting out. Learn the basics, build better habits and make smarter decisions about your finances.

💡 Start with the basics

You don't need to be a financial expert. Start with a few simple habits.

📒 Budgeting

A budget simply shows where your money comes from and where it goes. Tracking your spending makes it easier to avoid unnecessary expenses.

🐷 Saving

Even small amounts can add up over time. Try setting aside part of any money you receive before spending the rest.

🛍️ Needs vs Wants

Before buying something, ask yourself whether you actually need it or simply want it. This small habit can prevent impulse spending.

🛟 Emergency Fund

Unexpected expenses happen. Building a small emergency fund can give you some breathing room when something goes wrong.

🎓 Student Finances

Whether you're managing hostel expenses, transport, food or study costs, keeping track of regular expenses can make student life easier.

💻 Earning Skills

Learning useful skills can open doors to internships, freelance work, part-time jobs and other legitimate ways to earn.

🌱 Start small

Saving doesn't have to mean putting away a huge amount every month. Even a small percentage of your income can become useful over time.

Try setting a realistic saving goal and treating it like one of your regular expenses.

📌 Money habits worth building

Simple habits now can make future decisions easier.

1

Track your spending

You can't manage your money properly if you don't know where it's going.

2

Don't spend everything you receive

Try to keep something aside whenever possible, even if the amount is small.

3

Think before impulse purchases

Give yourself some time before making unnecessary purchases.

4

Learn before investing

Never put money into something simply because someone online says you'll make easy money.

5

Be careful with debt

Understand how much you'll have to repay before borrowing money or using credit.
