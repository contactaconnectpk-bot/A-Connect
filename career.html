📋 Build your career step by step

You don't have to do everything at once.

1

Know yourself

Think about your interests, strengths, values and the kind of environment you would like to work in.

2

Explore different careers

Don't choose a career just because everyone around you is choosing it. Research what different careers are actually like.

3

Build useful skills

Pick skills that match the career paths you're interested in and practise them through courses and projects.

4

Get some experience

Look for internships, volunteering, competitions, projects or other ways to gain practical experience.

5

Make your CV

Keep your CV simple and focus on relevant education, skills, projects, experience and achievements.

6

Keep improving

Your first career choice doesn't have to be your final one. Keep learning and adjusting as you gain experience.

🎤 Getting ready for an interview?

A little preparation can make a huge difference.

📝 Research the organisation

Know what the organisation does, what it values and what role you're applying for.

💬 Practise your answers

Prepare examples that show your skills, teamwork, problem-solving and achievements.

👔 Keep it professional

Be on time, communicate clearly and show genuine interest in the opportunity.
