📝 A few things that actually help

No complicated productivity system required.

⏱️ Work in focused sessions

Pick one task and give it your full attention. Use the timer above to stay on track.

📅 Break things down

Instead of writing "study chemistry", choose something specific like "finish chapter 3 notes".

🧠 Don't just reread

Close your notes and try to explain what you just learned. If you can't, that's the part you need to review.

🔄 Give your brain time

Revisiting a difficult topic over several days is usually more useful than trying to memorize everything in one night.
